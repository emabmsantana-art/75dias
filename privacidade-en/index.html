Privacy policy

75 Dias · updated 24 August 2026
Português · English · Español

75 Dias was built to work without a server. Your photos, your weight, your measurements, your journal and your meal plan stay on your phone and are not sent anywhere.

This policy explains the only three exceptions, what each one sends, and how to turn them off.

What stays on your phone

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private area, and is included in your iPhone's own iCloud backup — which belongs to Apple and to you, not to us:

  • your progress photos and every other photo you take in the app
  • your weight, body measurements and daily history
  • your journal, notes and answers to the daily questions
  • your workouts, including any imported from Apple Health
  • your saved meal plan, meals and shopping list
  • your name, if you enter one, and the app's settings

There are no user accounts, no sign-up and no password, and we cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes this data with it.

What leaves your phone

Three things, and none of them carries your name or anything that identifies you:

WhatWhere toWhen
The text of your meal plan A server of ours (Cloudflare), which passes it to Anthropic Only if you use "Read online", and only after you say yes
A product barcode Open Food Facts Only when you scan a barcode
Purchase data RevenueCat and Apple Only if you subscribe

1. Reading your plan online

You can write or paste your meal plan and ask the app to read it for you. When you do, the text of the plan — or the text read from a photo or PDF — is sent to a server of ours, which passes it to Anthropic to be interpreted, and returns the meals already structured.

The app asks you before the first time and explains this. If you say no, nothing is sent — and the app can still read your plan with no connection at all, using the "Read here" button. Your answer is saved and you can change it in Settings.

What is sent is only the plan text: foods and amounts. Your name, your weight, your photos and any identifier of yours are not included. Neither we nor Anthropic keep that text to train models.

2. Barcodes

If you scan a product barcode, the code is sent to the public Open Food Facts database to fetch nutrition values. It is a product code — it says nothing about you.

3. Subscription

Purchases are handled by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which creates an anonymous identifier per installation so it knows the subscription is valid. We never receive your name or your payment details — Apple does not give them to us.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhat for
CameraTaking the daily photo and scanning barcodes. Photos stay on the phone.
PhotosPicking a photo you already took, and saving a copy of your photos to the "75 Dias" album in your camera roll.
NotificationsThe reminders you choose. You can decline, and the app still works.
Apple HealthReading your workouts, if you want to import them. They are only read, they stay on the phone, and they are never sent anywhere. The app writes nothing to Health.

All are optional and requested at the moment they are needed — never on launch. You can decline or withdraw any of them in your iPhone's Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics, no usage statistics, no tracking.
  • It does not follow you across other apps or websites, and it does not show Apple's tracking prompt because it has nothing to track.
  • It does not sell or share your data. There is nothing to sell: we do not have it.
  • It does not compare your results with other people and has no leaderboards.

Your data is yours

  • Export: in Settings you can export everything — the app state as JSON and your photos, in a ZIP file you can keep wherever you like.
  • Delete: deleting the app deletes everything on the phone. In Settings you can also delete photos by type, or restart the challenge.
  • Ask us: because we keep nothing of our own about you, we have no data of yours to hand over or delete on request. If you have questions, write to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If any of this changes — for example, if the app one day gains accounts or sync between devices — this page is updated with the new date, and the app tells you before any new data is sent.
