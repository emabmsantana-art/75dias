Common questions

I missed a day. Do I lose everything?

It depends on the challenge you chose. In 75 Days, 75 Days medium and the 30-day one, missing a day goes back to day 1 — that's the rule. In 75 Days light, the day stays unfinished and the challenge carries on.

Either way, nothing you did is lost: the photos, workouts, weight and journal all stay, and the app keeps the restart as part of your story, with room to write what changes this time.

How do I make a backup?

In the app's Settings: Export backup (data only) or Export everything (with photos), which makes a ZIP with the photos inside. Keep the file wherever you like — Files, email, cloud.

Your iPhone's own backup already includes everything too. Lose the phone, restore a new one from it, and the app comes back as it was.

I'm changing phones.

On the old one, Export everything (with photos). On the new one, install the app and use Import backup. Careful: importing replaces whatever is in the new app.

I paid and the app still asks me to subscribe.

In Settings, tap Restore purchase. It has to be the same Apple ID you bought with. If it still doesn't come back, write to me with the date of the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancelling happens on Apple's side, not in the app: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or here. You keep everything until the end of the period you already paid for.

My Apple Health workouts don't show up.

Go to iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → 75 Days and check that reading workouts is switched on. The app only reads workouts — it never writes anything to Health.

How do I get my meal plan into the app?

In Food → My plan there are two ways. If you already have a plan from your dietitian, use Take my plan to my own AI: copy the request, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini with your PDF or photo attached, and bring the whole answer back to Paste the plan text. If you have no plan, the app writes one from a few questions.

The app is not a dietitian: it organises what you give it, and the numbers carry a margin of error. Check with a professional before big changes.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no password. It opens and it works.

I want to delete everything.

Deleting the app deletes everything on the phone — there's no copy on our side, because there's no server holding your data.

What languages is it in?

Portuguese, English and Spanish. The app follows your iPhone's language, and you can change it in Settings.
